# One Line, Big Laugh

Entry for the make.fun competition "One Line, Big Laugh" (`u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857`): a single hilarious line, at most 200 characters, that lands entirely on its own.

## The entry

> I asked ChatGPT to roast me, and it just sent back my screen time.

Why this line: the setup promises a witty insult, and the turn is that the AI doesn't even bother roasting — it just weaponises my own data against me. Short, fresh, and quotable.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the entry line byte-for-byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
